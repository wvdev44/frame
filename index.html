The library

All of our features

We set the standard where others charge extra. Enjoy an unmatched lineup of options, including exclusive features our competitors still put behind a paywall.

Alerts

Follows, subs, bits, and donations — animated toasts that queue cleanly instead of stacking on top of each other.

Webcam Frames

Circle, hex, or custom border shapes with reactive glow that syncs to your mic input.

Chat Widgets

A cleaned-up chat feed on your stream, with badges and emotes rendered exactly like Twitch.

Goal Bars

Sub, follower, and donation goals with a fill animation that never stutters mid-stream.

Countdown Timers

Be-right-back and starting-soon screens with a timer that keeps running even if OBS reconnects.

Scene Transitions

Wipes and stingers you can trigger from a hotkey, matched to your overlay's exact color and shape language.

Stream Labels

Clean, auto-updating text feeds displaying your newest follower, recent sub, and top tippers.

Now Playing

Real-time track titles and album artwork synced directly from Spotify, Apple Music, or local audio.

Sponsor Banners

Rotating carousel or static graphic frames displaying sponsor logos and discount codes cleanly.

Emote Wall

Particle visualizer that spawns floating chat emotes across the screen during hype moments.

Hype Train Tracker

A dynamic level meter tracking Twitch Hype Trains and channel sub pushes in real time.

Social Ticker

Minimalist cycling bar showcasing your Twitter, Discord, Instagram, and YouTube handles.

Polls & Predictions

Live visual bar graphs reflecting chat voting, viewer predictions, and channel point bets.

Input Visualizer

Real-time controller and keyboard visualizer highlighting button presses for speedruns and gaming.

Stream Health

A subtle overlay displaying active viewer counts, current stream uptime, and target FPS.

Tip Jar

Interactive 2D physics container where incoming bits and donations drop as falling coins.

Audio Visualizer

Responsive frequency wave bars that pulse dynamically to your mic input or stream music.

Schedule Calendar

Modular calendar widget displaying your upcoming stream dates, start times, and planned games.

Raid Boss Bar

Gamified health bar where chat interactions, subs, and bit donations deal damage to a custom boss.

Spin Wheel

Interactive giveaway wheel populated directly from chat participants or sub lists.

Discord Voice

Sleek voice chat widget showing active speakers and avatars without cluttering your gameplay.

Avatar Frame

Reactive avatar stage that highlights or switches avatar states seamlessly when speaking.

Chat Mini-Games

On-screen interactive widgets for chat commands like trivia, races, and mini-battles.

Clock & Uptime

Subtle digital clock readout displaying stream duration and local time zones for viewers.

Intermission Card

Modular panel showing stream highlights, social links, and estimated return times during breaks.

Live-Delay

Add a widget to show your delay, live status, if you're streaming a vod, or more.

Workflow

Three steps. No timeline, no keyframes.

SCENE 01

Pick your overlays

Browse the library and add whatever your layout needs — nothing pre-bundled, nothing you have to delete later.

SCENE 02

Drag, resize, position

Everything on your canvas snaps to a grid and to your other elements, so your layout stays pixel-aligned.

SCENE 03

Save & go live

Export straight into OBS or Streamlabs as a browser source. One link, no re-uploading files.
